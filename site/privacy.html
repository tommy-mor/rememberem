Remember'em privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Remember'em (Rememberem) helps you remember the people you meet: a photo, their name, where you met and what matters to them. It is made by Eric Jorgensen. This policy explains what the app keeps, where it is kept and what can leave your phone.

The short version

  • No account, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of our own.
  • Everything you save is kept in Remember'em's private storage on your phone. The developer never receives it.
  • If your phone backs up to Google, your Remember'em data is included in that backup.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • Photos of the people you choose to remember, cropped to head and shoulders. They are never saved to your gallery.
  • Names, notes and details you type or dictate, and practice scores.
  • Where and when you met: the time, the place name you give, and the GPS location of the phone or of a chosen photo. Location helps you remember a face by where you met, for example "I met them at the ball game".
  • A contact link, if you add one: only the phone's lookup key for the contact you pick. No contact details are copied.
  • Your settings and the reminder time.

This data is used only to show your people back to you, run the practice games and send the reminder you set. It is not used for advertising, sold or shared.

Faces

The app finds faces on the phone, using Google's ML Kit running on the device, only to crop each photo around the face. It does not create face templates, recognise or identify anyone, or compare faces. Nothing about a face is sent anywhere. Google's ML Kit and Android may send Google technical diagnostics, such as the device model, app version and performance data, never photos, names or notes.

What can leave your phone

  • Google backup. Android's backup is on for Remember'em, so if your phone backs up to Google, your photos and notes are included, and you can get your people back on a new phone. Google stores that backup under its own terms. To leave Remember'em out, turn off backup in your phone's Settings.
  • Dictation. When you speak instead of typing, the app asks your phone to recognise speech on the device. Where the phone has no on-device recogniser, your phone's speech service (for example Google) may send the audio to be recognised.
  • Street maps. Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap over an encrypted connection. OpenStreetMap sees your internet address and the area shown, never names, photos or notes. You can turn street maps off in Settings.
  • Links in the About page open in your browser or email app.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take the photo.
  • Microphone: for dictation, only while you are speaking.
  • Location: to record where you met.
  • Photo location: to read where a photo you choose was taken, so you can remember a face by the place you met.
  • Notifications: for the practice reminder and anniversary notices. Neither names anyone.

Every permission is optional. Without one, that feature is simply not used. Contacts are chosen through your phone's own contact picker, so the app needs no access to your contact list.

The people in your photos

You are responsible for asking before you photograph someone, and for keeping your notes kind enough that they could read them. Their photo and notes are in your phone's Google backup if that is on; if someone asks where their photo is kept, tell them. If someone says no after the photo is taken, tap Delete now on their page, just after the photo. It removes them from the phone straight away; a copy already in your Google backup may stay until the phone next backs up. Please don't use Remember'em to keep records about children.

Children

Remember'em is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Deleting your data

Move a person to the trash in the app and empty it to delete their record and photos. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Settings, deletes everything on the phone. Any copy in your Google backup is managed in your Google account.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Privacy questions and requests: rememberem@niftibits.com.
