Remember'em

Remember the people you meet.

For Android. Coming soon to Google Play.

How it works

  • Meet someoneOne tap opens the camera. The photo is cropped to head and shoulders.
  • Say what mattersSpeak or type their name, where you met and anything worth remembering. It saves as you go.
  • PracticeShort games with faces and details help the names stick, with a gentle reminder you can turn off.

Your people stay yours

No account, no ads, and no analytics of our own. Photos and notes are kept in Remember'em's private storage on your phone, and in your phone's Google backup if that is on. Dictation asks your phone to recognise speech on the phone where it can, and street maps load from OpenStreetMap (you can turn maps off). The privacy policy has the details.

Ask first

Every record is about a real person. Please ask before you take someone's photo ("Mind if I take a photo so I remember your name?"), and keep your notes kind enough that they could read them.
